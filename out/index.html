07 / FAQ

Questions?

Do I need prior quantum computing experience?+

No. The event is designed to welcome beginners as well as participants with previous quantum computing experience.

Do I need to know programming?+

Basic programming knowledge can be helpful, but beginner-friendly guidance will be provided.

What should I bring?+

Bring your laptop, charger and curiosity. Official requirements will be announced before the event.
